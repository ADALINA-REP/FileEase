100% On-Device & Zero Cloud Storage Guarantee

FileEase is architected as an offline-first tool. All image compression, resizing, format conversion, watermarking, and PDF document generation are executed entirely on your local device. We do not upload, host, view, or store your photos or documents on any remote server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application FileEase (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), available on the Google Play Store under package ID com.fileease.pdf.image.toolkit.

We value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your personal data and digital privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use FileEase, how that data is handled, and your privacy rights under global data protection laws (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR, and California Consumer Privacy Act - CCPA).

2. Zero Collection of Personal Files & Documents

Unlike online conversion services, FileEase conducts all computing operations locally through on-device native libraries:

  • Images & Photos: When you compress, resize, crop, or apply watermarks to your pictures, the files remain strictly in your device's memory and local sandboxed storage.
  • PDF Documents: When you convert photos to PDF, the rendering happens exclusively on your device.
  • No Remote Processing: No photos, identity cards, invoices, receipts, or generated documents are ever uploaded to an external server or cloud network.

4. Device Permissions We Request

FileEase requests only the minimum essential permissions necessary to operate:

5. Data Retention & Cache Management

FileEase does not maintain user accounts, profiles, or server databases. Processed files are saved directly to your device storage in the folders you authorize.

The App includes a built-in "Clean Cache" feature in the Settings screen, allowing you to instantly clear temporary files and thumbnails stored locally in the application cache.
